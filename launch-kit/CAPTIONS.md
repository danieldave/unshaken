# Unshaken launch week: status images and captions

Post one image a day to your WhatsApp status, Instagram story or TikTok. Paste the caption with your link (your Captain link if you have one).
Best time: early morning (6–8am) or evening (7–9pm). On day 3, post before 1 Dec.

Replace `[link]` with your site address, e.g. your Vercel link or domain.
